About SR Mattress

Quality sleep, every night – that is the promise behind every product we make.

Our Story

Making sleep better, one mattress at a time

SR Mattress is a mattress and pillow brand focused on one simple idea: everyone deserves soft, comfortable and durable sleep without paying a premium price.

We design our products around what families actually need – ultra-soft fabric that feels gentle on the skin, breathable material for cool nights, and a strong edge that keeps the mattress in shape for years.

Whether you are furnishing one bedroom or stocking a whole shop, hotel or hostel, SR Mattress supplies the same careful quality – at retail and at wholesale.
